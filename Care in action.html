Care In Action

Care In Action is an initiative that turns compassion into practical support by helping people in need. It focuses on providing food parcels to individuals and families facing hunger while promoting kindness and social responsibility.

Vision and Mission Statement

Care in Action aims to build a compassionate community where no one goes hungry by providing food parcels to those in need. We are committed to turning empathy into practical support through the collection and distribution of essential food items. Our goal is to promote dignity, kindness, and social responsibility while making a meaningful difference in people's lives.

Typical Content of Food Parcels

The food parcels provided by Care in Action are designed to supply a balanced range of essential nutrients needed for good health.

  • Carbohydrates: Rice, maize meal, and pasta for energy
  • Proteins: Beans, lentils, canned fish, or peanut butter for growth and body repair
  • Healthy Fats: Cooking oil or spreads for energy storage and brain function
  • Vitamins & Minerals: Canned vegetables and fruits for immune system support
  • Fibre: Whole grains and legumes for digestive health

Altogether, these nutrients help ensure that individuals and families receive a balanced diet that supports their physical health, energy levels, and well-being.

👤 Who Qualifies for Food Parcels?

This project is designed to support university students who are financially vulnerable, particularly those who are not receiving funding from bursaries or the National Student Financial Aid Scheme (NSFAS).

To ensure that assistance reaches those who need it most, students are required to provide valid proof from their student portal confirming that they are not funded. This verification process helps maintain fairness and transparency while allowing Care in Action to effectively provide food parcels to students facing financial challenges.

📋 Procedure to Receive Support

1

Application Submission

Students must apply for assistance by completing a simple request form provided by the Care in Action team.

2

Proof of Eligibility

Applicants are required to submit proof from their student portal confirming that they are not funded by any bursary or NSFAS.

3

Verification Process

The submitted documents will be reviewed to ensure that the student qualifies for support.

4

Approval Notification

Successful applicants will be notified through email or messaging platforms with details about collection.

5

Collection of Food Parcel

Students must collect their food parcels at the designated time and location, and may be required to present their student ID for confirmation.

📅 Distribution Schedule

Food parcels are distributed every Tuesday and Thursday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM at the Student Support Center. No appointment is needed—just bring your student ID and proof of residence.
