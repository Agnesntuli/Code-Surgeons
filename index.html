Understanding Menstruation

A menstrual cycle is a monthly process in the female body involving the shedding of the uterine lining. It typically lasts 3-7 days and is a normal, healthy part of reproductive health.

Types of Menstrual Products

  • Sanitary Pads - Absorbent products for leakage protection
  • Reusable Cloth Pads - Eco-friendly, washable options
  • Tampons - Internal absorption for active lifestyles
  • Menstrual Cups - Sustainable, long-lasting solution

About Us

Building bridges, breaking barriers, and creating opportunities

Our Mission

The Resources & Information Scarcity Platform was founded with a simple yet powerful mission: to ensure that no university student goes without essential resources due to financial constraints.

We believe that access to food, hygiene products, and educational support should not be privileges but fundamental rights for all students pursuing higher education.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every student, regardless of their financial background, has equal access to the resources they need to succeed academically and maintain their dignity and wellbeing.

Through technology, community partnerships, and dedicated volunteers, we are building a sustainable support system that empowers students to focus on their education rather than their basic survival needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and programs

Who is eligible for food parcels? +

Food parcels are available to all registered university students who can demonstrate financial need. This includes self-funded students, those awaiting funding, and students experiencing unexpected financial hardship. You will need to provide proof of student status and may be asked to provide documentation of your financial situation.

How often can I receive a food parcel? +

Students can receive one food parcel per week. Each parcel is designed to supplement your nutritional needs for approximately 5-7 days. In emergency situations, additional support may be available - please contact our team to discuss your specific circumstances.

Are menstrual products really free? +

Yes, all menstrual products are provided completely free of charge to any student who needs them. There are no eligibility requirements, no questions asked. We believe that access to menstrual products is a basic necessity and a matter of dignity.

How do I apply for emergency funding? +

Emergency funding applications are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact our office to schedule a confidential consultation with our financial aid advisor. Bring any relevant documentation of your emergency situation, and we will work with you to identify available resources and support options.

Can I volunteer if I'm not a student? +

Absolutely! We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds, including community members, alumni, and faculty. All volunteers undergo a brief orientation and are matched with roles that suit their skills and availability.

How can I donate food items instead of money? +

We gladly accept donations of non-perishable food items, personal hygiene products, and cleaning supplies. Please contact us to arrange a drop-off time. We particularly welcome rice, pasta, canned goods, peanut butter, cooking oil, soap, and toilet paper.

Is my information kept confidential? +

Yes, we take privacy very seriously. All information you provide is kept strictly confidential and is only used to facilitate the services you request. Your use of our services will not be disclosed to anyone outside our organization without your explicit consent.

What are your operating hours? +

Our main distribution center is open Tuesday and Thursday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The administrative office operates Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. For emergencies outside of these hours, please call our helpline at 068 261 3833.
